# Stock report layout — 2026-10-08

The stock detail is reorganized around a price header and evidence summary, followed by navigation to trend structure, volume and sentiment, valuation and expectations, company financials, event risk and historical price clues. Desktop uses two columns; narrow screens use one. Existing reference-price cards, confirmation paths, observations, research, official IR links and price-quality checks remain available.

New daily indicators are RSI14 with Wilder smoothing (14 initial changes; flat=50, all gains=100, all losses=0), 20 completed trading-week closing SMA, 52-week intraday OHLC range with 364-calendar-day coverage, daily close change and the latest SMA50/SMA200 crossing in available history. Crossings are labeled by type, date and trading-session age; no crossing is invented for an already-established initial relationship. The 52-week range is hidden unless full calendar coverage exists. Current indicators continue to require usable prices. RSI, weekly averages and range position do not change entry gates or establish cheap/expensive valuation.

The new /api/profile endpoint validates the Nasdaq summary symbol before reading industry and market cap. Earnings dates come from explicitly labeled overview table fields on Stock Analysis; invalid or past dates are rejected. All parsed future dates are conservatively marked estimated, with announcement timing unknown and company IR verification required. Company profile fields are source snapshots with fetch timestamps, not synchronized to close prices. ETF pages show constituent event and valuation requirements instead of company earnings or valuation assumptions. /api/fundamentals supplies existing sourced TTM and balance-sheet snapshots without claiming verified fiscal cutoff dates or growth rankings.

## 2026-10-09 Trend opening latency

The watchlist already contains the full technicalReview, but detail previously reset its numbers and waited for /api/market (which includes VIX, prices, research, and snapshot persistence). Detail now immediately renders the current watchlist review while other requests update independently. A market failure preserves those same-session indicators and leaves complete confirmation unknown. ## 2026-10-10 · Shared first paint and public access

- A returning browser paints its last successful watchlist immediately. A fresh browser independently reads `/api/watchlist?saved=1`, which uses the D1 summary snapshot or pre-existing observation records and never fetches upstream prices. Known symbols appear immediately even when no saved data exists. Live refresh runs separately with a 20-second client deadline; failures preserve the last displayed prices. Cached rows are labeled and market dates older than five days cannot supply price ranges.
- Cards now show the experimental SMA50-to-1.10×SMA50 range as “加仓观察区间 · 规则参考”. “到价” means price is inside the range; “接近” means within 3% of its nearest edge; “观察” covers other positions and unavailable/risk-blocked data. These are price positions only and do not change the existing technical or complete entry rules. Distance is measured relative to current close, with upward/downward direction stated. The lower boundary remains excluded.
- Holdings are opt-in per browser; no account holdings are imported or inferred, and marks are not sent to D1. Guests have their own marks.
- Public research writes require the owner email configured in the server RESEARCH_OWNER_EMAIL environment variable and a nonempty trusted Sites visitor ID. Without this environment setting, all writes are denied. Anonymous and other signed-in visitors are read-only. Same-origin, JSON limits, symbol allowlists, and prepared SQL remain enforced. The platform authenticates headers; the owner ID is Site-scoped and is not the workspace account ID. The D1 binding is server-only, not a browser database connection. Automated observation/price persistence remains server controlled.
